## ❓ Common Questions

**How do I play?**
Count the T-Rexes, tap your side of the screen once for each one, then **hold** to lock in your answer.
The fastest correct count wins the round. Babies, Triceratops, Raptors and Long-necks are tricks: check the
Get Ready screen to see what counts.

**What's free?**
- **Solo:** the whole Adventure and Solo Stampede, unlimited.
- **Duel:** free games every day.
- **Survival** and **two-player Stampede:** a few free games to try them.

One purchase, **Unlock everything**, removes every limit forever. No ads, no subscription.

**I bought Unlock everything but it's locked again.**
Open **Settings → Restore purchase** (or the Unlock screen → Restore purchase), using the same Apple Account
you bought with. The unlock also appears on your other devices with that Apple Account.

**My progress didn't move to my new device.**
Progress and records sync through **your own iCloud**. Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices
with iCloud turned on, open Raptap on both, and give it a minute.

**I can't hear any sounds.**
Raptap respects the silent switch. Turn silent mode off, check the volume, and make sure
**Settings → Sound effects** is on in the game.

**Does it work on iPad and iPhone Duo?**
Yes, in every orientation, Split View and Stage Manager. On iPhone Duo, unfold it and each player gets a whole screen.
