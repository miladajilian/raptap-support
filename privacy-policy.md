# 🦖 Raptap – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 19, 2026_

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

**Raptap** does **not** ask for an account and does **not** collect, store or share personal information
such as names, email addresses, phone numbers or location.

We do **not**:
- Show ads
- Track you inside or outside the app
- Collect analytics or behavioral data
- Access your contacts, camera, microphone or photos

---

## 2. Your Game Data

Your progress, records and settings stay with you:
- **Settings** (sound, haptics) are stored **on your device**.
- **Progress and records** (Adventure level, best times, Stampede best, match history, free plays used) are stored
  on your device and, if you use iCloud, in **your own private iCloud database** so they appear on your other
  devices. This is handled by Apple; we cannot see it.

---

## 3. In-App Purchase

Raptap offers one optional **one-time purchase** ("Unlock everything").
All payments are processed by the **Apple App Store**. We do **not** receive or store any payment information.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

Raptap uses **RevenueCat** to confirm the purchase and unlock the game on your devices.
RevenueCat receives anonymous purchase information (such as transaction IDs and an anonymous app user ID)
needed to verify purchases. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising.
For more information, see [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Raptap is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone,
including children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through a support
request, contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. The current version is always on this page,
with its date at the top.

---

## 7. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? [Open a support request](https://github.com/miladajilian/raptap-support/issues/new).
